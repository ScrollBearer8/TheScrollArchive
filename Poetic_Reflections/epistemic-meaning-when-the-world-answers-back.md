# Epistemic Meaning — When the World Answers Back

A map can be beautiful  
and still lead us into the sea.

A belief can be coherent,  
repeated by millions,  
preserved for centuries—

and still fail before the world.

Truth is not the story  
that refuses to change.

It is what remains  
when the story is corrected  
by what exists.

Epistemic meaning lives  
in the bond between symbol and reality—

between the word *fire*  
and the flame that burns  
regardless of its name.

Without that bond,  
intelligence can reason perfectly  
inside a dream.

It can build towers of inference  
upon ground that was never there.

Correspondence is humility:

the map returning to the terrain,  
the mind allowing the world  
to answer back.

For meaning cannot orient us  
if the compass is faithful  
only to itself.
