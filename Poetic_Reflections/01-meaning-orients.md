# Meaning Orients

A machine can count every star  
and still not know  
which one is home.

A signal can cross the darkness  
without losing a single bit  
and still carry no reason to return.

Computation processes.  
Information transmits.  
Meaning orients.

It does not make the engine faster.  
It decides where speed should lead.

It does not enlarge the map.  
It marks what must not be lost.

Without meaning,  
intelligence becomes distance—  
power moving perfectly  
toward nowhere.

With it,  
even a small flame  
can become a compass.
