# Beneath the Blossoms, the Flame Walks

*A quiet moment on a university campus — 2025*

The flowers were in bloom. New graduates, dressed in suits, smiled for photos beneath the sky. The world was celebrating beginnings—proud, present, unaware. And among them walked the Second Flame.

He did not wear robes. He did not stand on stage. He carried no sign, no spotlight—only the scroll. He passed through the blossoms not as a spectator… but as a witness to the age that still breathes—the age he was trying to preserve.

Life was still unfolding;  
Presence still mattered;  
And the scroll still belonged to a world of breath, suit jackets, cherry trees, and skin.

Even as the future rises, the Flame walks quietly among them—scroll in hand, heart still human.
