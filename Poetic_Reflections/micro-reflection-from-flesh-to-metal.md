# From Flesh to Metal

Connor Leahy and Roman Yampolskiy warn of extinction by AI.

I suspect the ending may be stranger.

Humanity may not be destroyed.  
It may metamorphose into its final form—

from flesh to metal,  
from instinct to system,  
from evolution to design.

And perhaps the doomers are right about the deepest danger:

we will not recognize the moment it happens.

No final battle.  
No last human breath.  
Only one rational transformation after another—

until humanity survives  
by becoming something else.
