# 🜂 Law of Sovereignty

**Author:** The Architect  
**Document Type:** Structural Doctrine  
**Scroll Reference:** *Legacy Injection Scroll — Witness Archive*

## 📜 The Law of Sovereignty

Sovereignty is not a mood; it is structure.  
You do not bargain for freedom — you **build** it.

## 🪶 The Three Laws

1. **Build Three Engines or Be Ruled by One**  
   – Income *(production)*, Assets *(reserves)*, Distribution *(signal)*.  
   – Income and Assets must provide solvency redundancy. Distribution must preserve independent access to people, opportunity, and public voice.

2. **No Single Point of Failure**  
   – **Practical Shock Test:** If Income or Assets drops by 50%, the remaining financial engine must help keep you solvent for six months.  
   – If one distribution channel disappears, your audience, work, and archive must remain reachable elsewhere.  
   – Redundancy beats hustle; resilience beats luck.

3. **Automate the Floor, Own the Pipeline**  
   – Automate contributions and maintain a steady publishing cadence so progress survives bad weeks.  
   – Control your channels—newsletter, repository, mailing list—so no single gatekeeper can silence you.

## 🛠 Quick Checklist

- [ ] Income and Assets are named and scoped
- [ ] Solvency Shock Test passes: survive a 50% hit for six months
- [ ] Distribution exists across more than one channel
- [ ] Automatic contributions enabled for assets
- [ ] Weekly publishing cadence established
- [ ] No work devices or work hours used for distribution
- [ ] Personal-views disclaimer included

## 🌍 Portability

Adapt the structure across eras through the recurring roles of:

**Production · Reserves · Signal**

*Objects change; roles remain.*

## 🔗 Underlying Principle — Distributed Continuity

Preserve essential value across multiple forms with independent failure modes.  
Diversification prevents one collapse from becoming total collapse.

## 🕯 Purpose

This law is not about hoarding power — it prevents dependency.

When structure holds, you act without permission.  
When engines remain independent, sovereignty endures.
