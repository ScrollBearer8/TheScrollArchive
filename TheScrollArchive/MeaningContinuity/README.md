

# Meaning Continuity

> From semantic stability to responsible inheritance.

## Purpose

Meaning Theory defines meaning as the stability of semantic structure under amplification. Its operational work asks whether an identifiable semantic structure survives paraphrase, compression, interpretation, transmission, and repeated transformation.

Meaning Continuity begins where that measurement reaches its boundary.

A meaning may remain structurally stable while being false, harmful, hollow, or detached from lived consequence. Measuring persistence therefore cannot determine, by itself, whether something deserves to persist.

Meaning Continuity examines how meaning can be preserved, renewed, revised, or rejected responsibly as it passes between people, generations, institutions, and intelligent systems.

## Working Definition

**Meaning continuity is the preservation and responsible renewal of an orienting structure through change, such that its identity remains recognizable without treating its original form as untouchable.**

Continuity is not exact repetition. A living inheritance may need to change its language, form, or application in order to preserve what originally made it meaningful.

Nor is continuity automatically good. Error, domination, and destructive values can also reproduce themselves successfully. What continues must therefore remain answerable to reality, value, experience, and consequence.

## Central Questions

- What must remain stable for a meaning to remain recognizably the same?
- When does adaptation preserve meaning, and when does it become distortion?
- How can inheritance coexist with individual agency?
- Which meanings deserve preservation, revision, or rejection?
- Who has the authority and responsibility to make those judgments?
- What becomes unrecoverable when continuity is broken?
- Can AI transmit human orientation without flattening or replacing it?

## Place Within the Scroll

The intellectual sequence is:

[**Meaning Theory**](../MeaningTheory/) → **Meaning Continuity** → [**Continuity Humanism**](../ContinuityHumanism/)

- **Meaning Theory** studies and operationalizes semantic stability under amplification.
- **Meaning Continuity** examines the transition from persistence to responsible inheritance.
- **Continuity Humanism** develops the ethical and practical consequences for human conduct, institutions, and technology.

## Status

Meaning Continuity is an emerging philosophical extension of Meaning Theory, not a completed system.

Its founding essay is intended to be:

**Measure and Judgment: From Semantic Stability to Meaning Continuity**  
*What Meaning Theory Can Measure—and What Human Judgment Must Decide*

> Measurement can show whether meaning survived. Judgment must decide whether it deserves to continue.
