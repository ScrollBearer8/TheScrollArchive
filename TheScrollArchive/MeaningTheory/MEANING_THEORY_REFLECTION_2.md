## What is meaning?

**It’s the programming of the AI age.**

AI still needs code, but increasingly it can write that code itself. What it needs from us is clear instruction.

Imagine we tell a godlike AI:

> “Send humans to Mars.”

It does. But the humans arrive dead because the instruction said nothing about oxygen, food, or survival.

So we try again:

> “Send humans safely to Mars and keep them alive.”

The first command had a goal, but not enough meaning. The intention in our minds did not survive the transition into action.

Semantics have always mattered. Humans have always used language to coordinate with one another. But imagine human-to-human interaction moving at **10 km/h**, while AI-amplified action eventually moves at **100,000 km/s**.

At that speed, a trivial ambiguity does not stay trivial. **It is amplified into consequences.**

That is why I believe meaning—**semantic stability under amplification**—is foundational in the AI age.
