## Aphorism I — Orientation

These formulations emerged from earlier work in Meaning Theory on the relationship between computation, information, and meaning. They are preserved here as three expressions of the same underlying idea.

### Version I — Condensed Form

> Computation processes.  
> Information transmits.  
> Meaning orients.

### Version II — Expanded Form

> Computation processes what information transmits.  
> Meaning orients what intelligence becomes.

### Version III — Canonical Form

> Computation processes.  
> Information transmits.  
> Meaning orients what intelligence becomes.

The canonical form combines the triadic rhythm of the condensed version with the complete claim of the expanded version.
