

# Continuity Humanism

> Human responsibility for what power carries forward.

## Preface

These are working principles arrived at through experience, reflection, and sustained engagement with technological change. They are offered for consideration rather than asserted as final truths. Each principle should be judged by what it clarifies, what it demands, and what following it would cost. Together they form a developing practical philosophy, not a closed moral system. Revision is permitted wherever reality or consequence proves a principle inadequate.

## Core Orientation

**Continuity Humanism is the philosophy that technological and civilizational progress should preserve and renew truth-grounded, value-bearing human orientation, while human beings retain responsibility for the ends toward which power is directed.**

Its central concern is not merely whether humanity can produce greater intelligence, speed, abundance, or control. It is whether those powers remain oriented toward anything worth becoming.

Human beings may delegate processes to machines. They cannot delegate responsibility for choosing the purposes those processes serve.

## What the Name Means

**Continuity** does not mean preserving every inherited belief or institution. It means carrying forward what remains worthy while allowing its form to change. An inheritance that cannot answer to truth or moral scrutiny may require revision or refusal.

**Humanism** does not mean hostility toward AI or the belief that technology has no place in human development. It means that technological capability does not relieve human beings of judgment, responsibility, dignity, agency, or care.

Progress without continuity may increase power while dissolving orientation. Continuity without revision may preserve the form of an inheritance after its meaning has died.

## Form of the Work

Continuity Humanism will be developed through short numbered precepts written in plain language.

Each precept should:

- Make a concrete claim about how to act
- Identify the value or responsibility at stake
- Name the cost, sacrifice, or tradeoff involved
- Remain understandable without specialized terminology
- Be open to disagreement, criticism, and revision

The precepts may be published individually or in small groups before being assembled into a stable collection. Reader response may expose unclear language or neglected consequences, but popularity will not determine their truth.

## Place Within the Scroll

The intellectual sequence is:

[**Meaning Theory**](../MeaningTheory/) → [**Meaning Continuity**](../MeaningContinuity/) → **Continuity Humanism**

Meaning Theory investigates whether semantic orientation survives amplification. Meaning Continuity asks what deserves to survive and how it can remain alive through change. Continuity Humanism translates those questions into conduct.

> Know what matters. Orient power toward it. Carry it forward without surrendering responsibility for what it becomes.

## Status

Continuity Humanism is a developing practical philosophy. This folder records its formation; it does not claim that the philosophy is complete.
